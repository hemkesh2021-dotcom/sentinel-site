01 — What it is

A frame, a rule, a record.

A camera captures what happened. Sentinel connects the observation, the rule and the evidence on the hardware beside it.

  1. 01

    Start with the source.

    One RTSP stream enters the local pipeline. Every observation carries its frame's source time, the boot and the stream epoch, so a stale or mismatched result never drives a current decision.

    RTSP → stamped frames
  2. 02

    Follow what moves.

    People are detected and followed as tracks across frames, on the Jetson. Empty, fresh, stale and offline states are published explicitly rather than guessed.

    Detect + track · on device
  3. 03

    Apply the rule.

    Zone rules decide what counts as an event. When one does, an incident opens in a local SQLite database, and alerts leave through a restart-safe outbox.

    Zone rules → SQLite incident
  4. 04

    Review it where it lives.

    A dashboard on the device's loopback address shows the live picture, the incidents and their context. You reach it through an SSH tunnel.

    127.0.0.1 · SSH tunnel

Optional lanes · experimental

Face recognition and questions answered by a local vision-language model exist as optional lanes. Neither has passed admission for normal use; they run in guarded validation runs only.

External alerts through Telegram are off by default. Turning them on is a separate, deliberate choice.

Evidence review

What an incident keeps.

The moment and the record of it, side by side: the frame as the camera saw it, and the few facts Sentinel writes down about it.

  • Freshness is visible.

    Fresh, stale, offline and empty states keep the age of an observation in view.

  • Context is connected.

    An incident carries the time and stream stamps of the observation behind it, so a record can be checked against what was seen.

  • Review stays local.

    The dashboard binds to localhost. Nothing about review requires an outside service.

    04 — Built in the open · v2.0.0-beta.1

    A beta, with clear boundaries.

    Sentinel is a project you can inspect, test and help improve. Start with the source and the limits of the current beta.

    Implemented

    The core one-camera path: detection, tracking, zone rules, local SQLite incidents, an alert outbox and a dashboard. Portable tests and a bounded device check exist. The system has no production acceptance.

    Experimental

    Face recognition and visual chat have not passed admission for normal use. The dashboard may show these panels. No accuracy, speed or reliability guarantee is claimed.

    Unverified

    The reference is a Jetson Orin Nano 8 GB. A complete launch from the current tester package, and any other Jetson configuration, remain unverified. Read the tester guide and release notes before trying the beta.

    What leaves the device

    Core inference and incident storage are local. External alert channels are disabled by default; enabling Telegram sends selected alert information to that external service. This website does not connect to your camera.
